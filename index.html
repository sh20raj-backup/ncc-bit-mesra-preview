About the unit

More than training.
A commitment to service.

The National Cadet Corps is a voluntary youth organisation that helps young people develop discipline, leadership, comradeship and a spirit of selfless service. At BIT Mesra, the technical company's history spans decades of change and continued cadet development.

Through structured training, ceremonial duties, camps and community initiatives, cadets have opportunities to practise responsibility, build teamwork and serve beyond the classroom.

“Unity and Discipline” — the motto of the National Cadet Corps.

From Sindri to Mesra

A legacy shaped
over generations.

The dates and designations below are based on the unit’s “Brief History” document supplied for this website concept. The evolution records its changing technical structure and identity.

05 JAN 1956

Raised at Sindri

Established as 6 Bihar EME Section NCC to provide NCC coverage to the Bihar Institute of Technology, Sindri.

01 JAN 1957

Moved to Mesra

The unit moved to its present location at Mesra, Ranchi, to provide NCC coverage to Birla Institute of Technology.

JUL 1964

Reorganised

The unit was reorganised with a reported cadet strength of 120 and redesignated 1 Bihar EME Unit, Mesra.

NOV 1967

Mixed Regiment formed

Engineers, Signals and EME technical companies were amalgamated. The historical document records a total strength of 600 cadets.

1977

Composite Technical Company

The regiment was reduced to a company, technical companies became platoons, and the reported cadet strength reduced to 200.

AFTER 2000

Jharkhand designation

Following formation of Jharkhand state, the unit was redesignated 3 Jharkhand Composite Technical Company NCC, Mesra.

Historical note: the designation “after 2000” is intentionally broad; the exact redesignation date has not been confirmed in the material reviewed.
